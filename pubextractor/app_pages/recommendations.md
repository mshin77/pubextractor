# Recommendations

Rules read the paper first; a model, when chosen, fills only what the rules leave open. The coder confirms or changes each recommendation.

| Recommendation | Based on |
|---|---|
| Design | design terms in the Method |
| RCT or QED | assignment terms |
| Arm role | label words or the other arm in the table |
| Graph | design, outcome, and goal words |
| Construct | matching or similar names (0.80) |

References: Shadish et al. (2002); What Works Clearinghouse (2022).
