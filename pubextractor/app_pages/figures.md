# Single-Case Graphs

Review → Figures reads points from graphs into rows for [scdbayes](https://github.com/mshin77/scdbayes).

## Steps

1. Pick a figure.
2. Calibrate: click two x ticks and two y ticks, and type their values.
3. Edit points: click to add a missed point, or click a point to remove it.
4. Under Study, check the suggested design, outcome type, and goal, then Save graph data.
5. Replot draws the saved rows to compare with the original.

Bar, forest, scatter, and flow figures are read the same way; pick the type under Study.

## Limits

Hollow or overlapping markers and dashed criterion lines need clicks. Compare a sample with [WebPlotDigitizer](https://automeris.io/) before analysis.
