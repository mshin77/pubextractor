# Tables and Effect Sizes

Tables become values with arm, outcome, time, and statistic; group-design tables become Hedges' g.

## Review

- Click a cell to correct it.
- Image tables (scanned tables and image files): type the values, or Read with vision model under the license check.
- Arm roles and Assignment are suggested; confirm or change them, then Compute effect sizes.

## Effect sizes

The first method the data allow, in the WWC order: adjusted difference, posttest difference, from t, from F, difference in differences. g and its variance follow the WWC Procedures and Standards Handbook 5.0. Values that fail a check leave g empty with the reason.

Export → metafor writes `yi` and `vi`; effects sharing a control share a `control_id`.

## Correlations

A correlation table is read as a matrix with its n. Map its labels to constructs in the Correlations card, then Export → Correlation matrices.
